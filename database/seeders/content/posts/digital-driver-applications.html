Paper applications slow hiring down. Drivers fill in forms by hand, pages go missing, and safety managers retype everything into a spreadsheet.

What changes with a digital application

  • Applicants finish on their phone. No printer, no fax, no app to install.
  • Consents are built in. FMCSA, PSP and drug and alcohol policy acknowledgements are signed as part of the flow.
  • Nothing is skipped. Required fields and documents have to be completed before the application can be submitted.
  • The file starts itself. Once hired, the applicant's information and documents are already in their Driver Qualification File.

The result

Faster hiring, fewer errors and a complete file from day one.
