A DOT compliance review can arrive with little warning. Carriers that are ready treat audit preparation as a habit, not a project.

1. Check every driver file

Open each Driver Qualification File and confirm that every required document is present and current. Pay special attention to medical certificates and annual reviews.

2. Review drug and alcohol testing records

Make sure pre-employment results, random selections and Clearinghouse queries are documented for every CDL driver.

3. Look at hours of service and vehicle maintenance

Auditors look beyond driver files. Inspection, repair and maintenance records should be organised by vehicle and easy to produce.

4. Fix gaps before the auditor finds them

If something is missing, correct it now and document what you did. A clear record of corrective action is far better than a surprise.

5. Make it continuous

Automated reminders and a single dashboard turn audit prep from a scramble into a quick review.
