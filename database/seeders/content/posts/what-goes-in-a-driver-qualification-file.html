Every motor carrier that operates commercial motor vehicles must keep a Driver Qualification File (DQF) for each driver. The requirement comes from 49 CFR Part 391, and it is one of the first things an auditor asks to see.

The core documents

  • Employment application with the information required by §391.21
  • Motor vehicle record (MVR) from each state where the driver held a licence in the past three years
  • Road test certificate, or an accepted equivalent such as a valid CDL
  • Medical examiner's certificate, kept current
  • Safety performance history requests to previous DOT-regulated employers
  • Annual MVR review and the record of that review
  • Clearinghouse queries for drivers who need a CDL

Where carriers slip up

Most violations aren't missing files; they are expired documents. A medical certificate that lapsed last month or an annual review that never happened counts against you.

Keeping it audit-ready

Track every expiry date in one place, set reminders well before the deadline, and keep each document with its driver. That is exactly what a digital DQF system does for you.
