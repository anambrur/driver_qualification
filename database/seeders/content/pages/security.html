Driver files contain some of the most sensitive data a carrier holds. This page explains how {site} protects it.

Data in transit

All traffic to {site} is served over HTTPS. Payment card details are entered on Stripe and never touch our servers.

Access control

  • Every company's data is kept separate from every other company's.
  • Role-based permissions decide who can see or change driver records, documents and billing.
  • Drivers can only see their own file.
  • Platform staff can only open a customer account through a time-limited support session the customer can see.

Audit trail

Important actions, such as changes to users, roles, documents and billing, are recorded with who did them and when.

Passwords and sign-in

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Applicants confirm their phone number with a one-time code before they start an application.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please tell us through the Contact page{email_line} before disclosing it publicly. Include enough detail for us to reproduce it. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated.
