This Privacy Policy explains how {site} ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or use our driver qualification platform.

Who this policy covers

  • Customers: motor carriers and their users who hold an account.
  • Drivers and applicants: people whose information a customer collects through the platform.
  • Visitors: people who browse our website or contact us.

For driver and applicant information, the carrier you apply to or drive for decides what is collected and why; we process it on the carrier's behalf.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone, company details and billing contact.
  • Driver qualification information: identity and contact details, date of birth, licence details, employment and driving history, medical certificate, motor vehicle records, drug and alcohol testing records, consents and signatures, and the documents uploaded to a driver file.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe. We store only a reference to your subscription, never full card numbers.
  • Usage information: log data such as IP address, browser and pages visited, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact or demo forms.

How we use information

  • To provide the platform and keep driver files complete and current
  • To send service emails and text messages, such as expiry reminders and one-time codes
  • To bill subscriptions and prevent fraud
  • To answer questions and support requests
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the platform (for example hosting, email, SMS and payments), with the carrier a driver applies to or works for, or where the law requires it.

Retention

Customer data is kept while the account is active and for a limited period afterwards so records can be exported, unless the law requires a longer period. Driver qualification records follow the retention periods in the FMCSA regulations.

Security

See our Security page for how we protect information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Drivers and applicants should contact the carrier first; we will help the carrier respond. Anyone can reach us through the Contact page{email_line}.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
